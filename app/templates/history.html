🏆 Hall of Fame

Honoring the greatest players in league history. Real induction over the full career-cumulative archive built from every completed season above — a player qualifies at QB, RB, WR/TE, or DEF once they've accrued at least 2 archived seasons of production and a composite score (career production, weighted by MVP/OPOY/DPOY/ROY awards actually won) clears the bar, not a fixed top-N. GDD-underspecified induction criteria; see history_store.py's own docstring for the exact formula. No Coach entity exists in the engine yet (Post-MVP), so this Hall is player-only for now.
