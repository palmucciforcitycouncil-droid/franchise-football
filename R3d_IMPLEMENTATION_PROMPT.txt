What you're building:

1. **Enhanced Job Security Score (JSS)** — multi-factor, context-aware (not just win-loss)
2. **OwnerWinPressure** — franchise-level, persistent, yearly adjustment based on expectations
3. **Firing Probability Model** — probabilistic (not deterministic), with in-season gates, tenure modifiers
4. **Replacement Logic** — separate from firing; decides WHO to hire once a coach is fired
5. **Coach Free-Agent Pool** — seeded from Top_100_Football_Coaching_Candidates.xlsx, with real ratings
6. **AI Autonomy** — all 31 non-user teams autonomously hire/fire/promote every offseason + in-season
7. **User Controls** — Hire/Fire/Promote buttons on Staff page for the user's own team only
8. **Emergent Narratives** — the system should naturally produce realistic coaching stories (patient owner + improving team survives; impatient owner + strong roster fires; coordinator failures trigger coordinator fires without HC firing; etc.)

What you're NOT building (disclosed scope cuts):
- Multi-team AI bidding wars for coaches (single deterministic verdict, like player FA)
- Coach salary negotiation (they accept/reject based on merit + interest, not haggling)
- Salary cap on coaching (no cap exists in real NFL)

Settled decisions (do not re-open):
1. AI teams get full autonomy, user controls their own team manually
2. Coach free-agent pool is seeded from real coaches (Excel file)
3. College coaches are rated LOWER than current NFL coaches (prevent wholesale replacement)
4. Big-school college HC will NOT accept interim-only or assistant roles
5. OwnerWinPressure does NOT reset when HC is fired (franchise expectations persist)
6. Build hiring/firing/promotion simultaneously, not in phases
